# Practice, projects, and local progress

Learners check their understanding, run editable Python exercises, complete portfolio projects, and keep their place on their own device.

## Who and why
For curious beginners and developers working toward AI engineering, with an emphasis on understanding through examples and practice.

## Learner workflow
Learners check their understanding, run editable Python exercises, complete portfolio projects, and keep their place on their own device.

## Included
Add quiz feedback with explanations, a sandboxed worker Python runner with timeout/reset and explicit download state, project briefs with datasets and acceptance rubrics, downloadable notebooks, bookmarks, local completion and notes, export/import progress. Validate corrupt storage, quiz scoring, execution timeout and worker lifecycle.

## Boundaries
No paid enrollment or identity requirement. No promises of employment or exhaustive coverage of active research. Third-party resources are linked with attribution.

## Acceptance
A learner can complete the described workflow with clear outcomes, understandable feedback, and usable mobile and keyboard interfaces. All implemented behavior is tested where practical and documented honestly.
