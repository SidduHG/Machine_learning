# Curriculum and lesson reader

Learners follow a coherent path from Python and mathematical foundations through classical ML, neural networks, and AI engineering. Each lesson combines intuition, equations, a worked example, code, practice, and further reading.

## Who and why

For curious beginners and developers working toward AI engineering, with an emphasis on understanding through examples and practice.

## Learner workflow

Learners follow a coherent path from Python and mathematical foundations through classical ML, neural networks, and AI engineering. Each lesson combines intuition, equations, a worked example, code, practice, and further reading.

## Included

Add typed modules and lessons in lib/curriculum, searchable curriculum route, dynamic lesson route, KaTeX math rendering, source links, printable notes, previous/next navigation, and lesson metadata. Include original topic-specific notes across the entire path.

## Boundaries

No paid enrollment or identity requirement. No promises of employment or exhaustive coverage of active research. Third-party resources are linked with attribution.

## Acceptance

A learner can complete the described workflow with clear outcomes, understandable feedback, and usable mobile and keyboard interfaces. All implemented behavior is tested where practical and documented honestly.
